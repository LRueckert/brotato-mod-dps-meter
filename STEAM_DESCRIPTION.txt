[h1]DmgMeter[/h1]
Per-wave Damage, Healing, and Materials Meters for Brotato.

[list]
[*][b]Damage:[/b] weapons, items, characters, and spawned Sources. Overkill Damage does not count.
Turrets, landmines, and traps count toward the weapon or item that spawned them: each Wrench, Crowbar, Screwdriver, Carbon Bow (Engineering arrows), or Awl shows its own Structures' damage, including burns from a Wrench's flame turrets. Bot-o-Mine shows the mines it lays, Pocket Factory shows its tree turrets, and the Turret, Blood Turret, Landmines, and Dart Trap items show only theirs.
[*][b]Healing:[/b] regeneration, lifesteal, consumables, and direct healing effects. Only actual HP restored counts.
[*][b]Materials:[/b] ground pickups, pickup bonuses, weapon/item-generated income, and the end-of-wave Harvesting payout. Harvesting includes its wave-end bonuses, counts toward the wave that just ended, and shows 0 when the payout is negative. Income no other Source claims (such as per-step or character-ability materials) is grouped into the Other Source; spending and discounts are excluded.
[/list]

Use [b]Q/E[/b] (configurable in Options) or a controller's [b]shoulder buttons/triggers[/b] to switch the Active Meter in each player's Meter Panel independently.

Totals are tracked per player, reset each wave, and sorted highest first.
Zero Totals are hidden; each Meter shows up to 20 Sources by default with one or two players, or six Sources with three or four players.
Material pickups follow the game's co-op sharing.
At the end of each wave, a Wave Snapshot of every Meter is available in the level-up and shop screens.

[h1]Options[/h1]
With [b]Oudstand-ModOptions[/b] installed, open [b]Options > Mods > DmgMeter[/b] to change options, otherwise default values will work fine.
Changes are saved automatically and apply immediately to all Meter Panels and players.

[list]
[*][b]Wave Snapshots:[/b] Show Wave Snapshots between rounds in the stats panel, default [b]enabled[/b].
[*][b]Percentage Bars:[/b] Show each Source's Total relative to the highest Total in its Meter, default [b]disabled[/b].
[*][b]Percentage Bar width during wave:[/b] Set the HUD Percentage Bar width from [b]50-280 px[/b] (capped on HP bar size), default [b]150 px[/b].
[*][b]Sources per Meter (1-2 players):[/b] [b]1-20[/b], default [b]20[/b].
[*][b]Sources per Meter (3-4 players):[/b] [b]1-20[/b], default [b]6[/b].
[*][b]Combine duplicate weapons:[/b] Show copies of the same weapon with the same tier, cursed state, and mechanized state as one Source with a "×N" count and their summed Total, in every Meter and in Wave Snapshots. Items, characters, and grouped Sources are never combined, and each player's weapons are combined separately. Default [b]disabled[/b].
[*][b]Previous Meter keybind / Next Meter keybind:[/b] Switch the Active Meter, default [b]Q[/b] / [b]E[/b]. Picked from a dropdown, because ModOptions doesn't offer a keybind option type.
[/list]
